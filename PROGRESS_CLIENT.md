## ✅ What's ready

| | Feature | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| 🏠 | **Welcome page** | Visitors land on a branded MinistryHub page, not a data dashboard. It shows a few headline facts about the ministry job market. |
| 📝 | **Sign-up before the report** | Visitors give their name, email and organization before they see a report. |
| 📊 | **The pay report** | A clear pay range for the role, how sure we are about it (Strong, Moderate or Limited), and pay by state, church size and denomination. |
| 🔎 | **Always gives an answer** | When a state doesn't have enough data, the tool looks wider (the region, then the whole country) and says so plainly. |
| ⏱️ | **Full-time and part-time kept apart** | Full-time roles show a yearly salary. Part-time roles show an hourly rate. They are never mixed. |
| ☑️ | **Pick several at once** | Visitors can choose more than one state, church size or denomination. |
| 💰 | **Check my offer** | Visitors type the salary they plan to offer and see how it compares with the market. |
| 📄 | **Branded download** | A MinistryHub-branded report that visitors can save as a PDF. |
| 🔗 | **Share with your board** | Visitors can copy a link or email it. Anyone who opens it signs up first, which brings you a new lead. |
| 🙈 | **Your sources stay private** | The public never sees job sites, employer names, links to postings, or exact numbers of jobs. |
| 🎨 | **MinistryHub branding** | Your colors, font, logo and "book a call" buttons throughout. |
| 🔐 | **Secure admin sign-in** | Each team member has their own login, and no password shows on screen. |

## 🛠️ What's still to do

### 1. Clean the data · *the biggest piece*
- Remove jobs that aren't ministry jobs, such as schools in towns called "Church Hill" or hospital roles.
- Count a job once, even when it was posted twice or reposted.
- Sort jobs into the roles churches actually search for, such as Campus Pastor, Student Pastor and Head of School.
- Tidy the denomination list, for example combining "Catholic" with "Roman Catholic".
- Find church size and denomination for more churches.
- Keep nonprofit jobs out of the public numbers for now, as you asked.

### 2. Turn visitors into leads
- Email the report to visitors automatically.
- Record what each visitor searched, changed, downloaded and clicked.
- Send Jordan an alert when someone looks like a warm lead.
- Add new sign-ups to Loxo (daily) and Mailchimp (weekly).

### 3. Admin tools
- A list of everyone who signed up, with their activity, which you can export.
- Screens where your team can correct a wrong decision made by the AI.
- A monthly spending limit for the AI.

### 4. Keep the data fresh
- Refresh the job data automatically on a schedule.
- Add more job sites once we have your list.
